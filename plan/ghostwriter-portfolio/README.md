# Ghostwriter Portfolio Handoff

This folder is the execution brief for turning Ghostwriter into a standout portfolio artifact.

The goal is not to build a startup. The goal is to ship a compact, high-taste AI micro-product that:

1. Explains itself in under 5 seconds.
2. Feels magical within 15 seconds.
3. Rewards deeper inspection with real product and engineering craft.

The four approved pillars are:

1. Live rewrite animation
2. Surprise Me
3. How It Works drawer
4. Case study page

## Product Thesis

Ghostwriter should feel like playful magic with visible craft.

- `Playful magic` means a visitor can click one button and instantly see something delightful happen.
- `Visible craft` means a technical reviewer can see prompt design, interaction design, security thinking, and implementation discipline.

## Non-Goals

- No auth
- No billing
- No teams
- No full doc editor
- No productivity workflow expansion
- No attempt to become Grammarly, Jasper, or Notion

## What “Done” Looks Like

A first-time visitor can:

1. Click `Surprise Me`
2. Watch a sentence get rewritten live
3. Share a polished permalink
4. Open `How It Works`
5. Read a concise case study and understand the product thinking
