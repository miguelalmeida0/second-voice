# Second Voice
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Rewrite a draft through a literary voice or a writing outcome, and compare the result with the original. Product requirements come from codex-duet-handoff/PROMPT.md and the existing application.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Four authors, five outcomes, three visible practical quick starts plus six original Quick Starts, three author-specific strengths, 2,000-character drafts, animated edit playback with skip and reduced motion, copy, optional Rewrite Lab, feedback and explicit public sharing. The initial right panel explains the flow. A full-width mode switch distinguishes Authors (match a writer’s voice) from Outcomes (improve your message). Changing settings never replaces the draft or an existing result and does not spend allowance. Surprise me and sample-preview actions are removed. Only explicit rewriting uses the existing allowance, which remains visible beside the action. Preserve feature flags, GitHub and invited email authentication, guest allowances, request protection, error handling, account deletion, privacy and legal routes.

## Brand Commitments
Second Voice. The user's Preview first image of 2026-10-05 is the current visual authority, superseding the original Duet control-plane composition. No new product claims or invented backend progress. No deployment or merge in this task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Native document scrolling, keyboard controls and dialog focus management. Responsive from 320 through 2560 pixels, practical 44px touch targets, visible focus and sufficient contrast.
